MY STORY

From education
to cultural making.

I studied Psychology and Education of Exceptional Children at the University of Tehran. Alongside university, I explored many roles — educational counseling, bookselling, public relations, fashion design assistance and running an online shop. Those experiences taught me how to listen, communicate, build and adapt.

In 2023, I founded Ariidoch — “stitched” in Balochi. I began rescuing embroidery from garments that could no longer be worn and bringing it back into circulation as jewelry and art. I also create mirror-work pieces from the first sketch to the final stitch.

THE PRACTICE
01

Recover

Identify embroidery fragments with cultural and material value, including pieces from well-worn garments.

02

Recompose

Study motif, age, color and wear; then design a contemporary form that respects the original language.

03

Make

Handcraft jewelry, mirror-work and framed pieces in limited editions or as unique works.

04

Retell

Give each object a name and a story so Balochi women’s visual language remains visible and alive.
